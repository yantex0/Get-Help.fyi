# About

---

The write-ups on **Get-Help.fyi** are written by a real person with real passion for IT who has real experience and that is something I am proud of. It means you can read any of these guides knowing its written with thought and care.

## Writing Criteria

---

In an attempt to keep consistency and usability of guides, I have set these criteria for myself:

{{% columns %}}

* ### 1. Quality not Quantity

    Write from my own personal experience as a person who has quite a few years of experience in IT.

* ### 2. Beginner Friendly

    Not assume too much about anyone's knowledge to keep the guides useable by most everyone.

{{% /columns %}}

{{% columns %}}

* ### 3. Short and Sharp

    Keep guides as short as possible, easy to follow and aren't full of filler.

* ### 4. Practical Information

    Make the information practical. Whether it be guides, scripts or anything else I might add.

{{% /columns %}}

## PowerShell Terminology Used

---

1. All guides are using **Windows 11**.
1. All guides are using [PowerShell 7.7+][ps-core].
1. All guides will use this terminology for both unique **PowerShell editions** as:

    | Edition            | Characteristics                             | Link                                     |
    | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
    | PowerShell         | Newer, actively maintained, cross platform. | [PowerShell][ps-core]                    |
    | Windows PowerShell | Older, deprecated, Windows only.            | [Windows PowerShell][Windows-PowerShell] |

1. All guides are using [Windows Terminal](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/terminal/).
