# Documentation about Documentation

---

The write-ups on **Get-Help.fyi** are written with thought and care and are not mass produced by an LLM and that is something I am proud of and I hope others can appreciate as well when reading (even if you might see a typo or two!).

## Writing Criteria

---

> [!NOTE]
> To maintain a high quality of guides over time, I have set these criteria for myself

### **1. Quality not Quantity**

Write based on my own years of experience in IT

### **2. Beginner Friendly**

Keep language simple to allow the guides to be usable by nearly anyone

### **3. Short and Sharp**

Keep guides short, easy to follow and low on filler whenever possible

### **4. Practical Information**

Keep the write-ups practical and usable
